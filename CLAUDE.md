# FlipUp: notes for Claude

Poker night on your phones: no-limit Texas Hold'em for play chips. A host opens a table and friends join on their own phones with a 5-letter code; bots fill empty seats. There's also a bots-only mode that runs entirely on one phone. Texas Hold'em has nine themed mini game variants beside it, each with its own look, and the campaign has two floor games: roulette and blackjack. Read `docs/RULES.md` before changing any poker rule.

## Commands

- `npm run dev`: app plus the Worker and Durable Object, all local (http://localhost:5173)
- `npm run typecheck`: `tsc -b` over app, worker and tooling projects
- `npm test`: Vitest unit tests for `src/game`
- `npm run e2e`: Playwright, phone-sized Chromium, starts its own dev server on 5174. In the cloud sandbox Chromium is at `/opt/pw-browsers` and `@playwright/test` is pinned to 1.56.1 to match it. `SHOTS_DIR=/some/dir` saves a screenshot of each screen. If Playwright can't find its browser (the installed one is a newer build), set `CHROMIUM_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`.
- `npm run check`: typecheck + unit tests + build. Run it before committing.
- `npm run deploy`: build and `wrangler deploy` (needs `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` and `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID` in the environment; never commit them).
- After editing `wrangler.jsonc`, run `npm run cf-typegen` and commit `worker-configuration.d.ts`.

## Layout

- `src/game/`: the pure TypeScript poker engine, shared by the app and the server. No DOM or Node APIs here.
  - `cards.ts`, `evaluate.ts` (hand ranking), `equity.ts` (odds and the bots' hand strength)
  - `holdem.ts`: dealing, blinds, betting rounds, side pots, showdown
  - `reducer.ts`: `applyAction()`, the only way a table changes. Server and phone run the same code.
  - `bots.ts`, `selectors.ts`, `validate.ts` (`parseAction()` for anything from the network), `redact.ts`
  - Bots: 40 named bots (`botStyles.ts`), four each of ten hidden styles, plus 600 "regulars" (`botProfiles.ts`). `botPlay.ts` decides: preflop from GTO charts (`botCharts.json`, 2–6 players at 100/40/20bb; 7–9 handed fold into the 6-max chart) bent by style, push/fold when short, and an equity check against the raiser's range for big raises and all-ins; postflop it imagines the opponents' hands from their public story (`botRange.ts`: preflop line plus every postflop move, weighted by how well each hand fits, adjusted by `game.reads`), then weighs check/bet sizes/call/raise/fold by equity, fold equity and what it wins when called, blended with a slice of GTO's mix from `botPostflop.json`. Styles set how much a bot reads ranges (`sense`), its leaks, bet-size tells, tempo and when it shows cards. `botEval.ts` is a fast evaluator (matches `scoreHand`) so a decision stays within a couple of ms on the server. `botMind.ts` keeps each bot's tilt, confidence and grudges in `game.minds`. `botTalk.ts` holds their fixed comment lines (ids `b:...`, never accepted from phones). A bot that speaks before the outcome ("c'mon!", a shove) is in `game.sweating` and always reacts when the hand ends (`afterHand()` in `reducer.ts`, `owed` skips the cooldown and the dice). On the phone, `useReactions()` in `src/screens/table/Bubbles.tsx` shows bot lines one at a time, holds outcome reactions back half a second to a second, and drops "c'mon" lines when the next card lands or the pot goes. `redactForDevice()` strips styles, reads, minds and plans. Rebuild the data with `gto/scripts/export_bot_charts.py` and `gto/scripts/train_bots.py` (its `--parity` output is `botPlay.parity.json`, which the unit test checks the TypeScript buckets against). `botSim.ts` plays bot tables (and scripted players, including a constant bluffer, `bluffBot`) for tuning; the bots note how often a player bets (`betFreq`) and call lighter against a constant bettor, and punish players who fold too much (`EXPLOIT` in `botPlay.ts`; see `docs/BOTS.md`); `scripts/botLab.ts` runs the full lab and fun score (see `docs/BOTS.md`), with the bots from before the update in `scripts/legacy/` to compare against. On top of the style numbers each style and legend has a `craft` (`Craft` in `botStyles.ts`): situational techniques (c-bets by board, barrels, floats, probes, donks, check-raises, polarised river bets, thin value, hero calls, lay-downs, 4-bet bluffs and light 3-bets, a signature hand, bet sizes that follow the board) that `botPlay.ts` applies as pulls in pots, and `exploit`, how hard it acts on the situational reads in `PlayerRead` (fold by street, to c-bets and to 3-bets, check-raise, limp, what their bet size has meant when shown down; recorded by `updateReads()`/`recordHabits()`, used through `exploitRead()` in `botRange.ts`). A twin's craft is always neutral (`NO_CRAFT`): it plays its person's habits. `scripts/craftLab.ts` (`freq`, `ab`, `single`, `ladder`) checks each technique shows up in play and earns its place (see `docs/BOTS.md`).
- FlipUp Premium (`src/state/premium.ts`, `screens/sheets/PremiumSheet.tsx`, `PremiumPickers.tsx`, `src/styles/premium.css`): a $9 one-time upgrade (`PREMIUM_PRICE`) that unlocks four things: eleven card packs (`CARD_PACKS` in `src/game/cardPacks.ts`: Gold Rush, Neon Grid, Aurora, Holo, Ruby Ember, Emerald, Sapphire, Obsidian, Sakura, Galaxy, Circuit; the venue trophy backs in the same list are won in the campaign instead; the back of your cards, each with its own colour and a shine that sweeps across. `Player.cardPack` is public, set through `setLook` like the bubble colour; `PlayingCard` takes `pack`, which paints the back and the back a card flips from (`.pk.pack-<id>` in `premium.css`). Everyone at an online table sees it on the back of your cards (Pods), and you see it on your own cards and the board as they flip. Picked in sheet `packs`: "Card packs" in Settings and the table menu), eleven chat bubble effects (`BUBBLE_STYLES` in `src/game/bubbles.ts`: Sunset, Gold, Aurora, Neon, Rainbow, Ocean, Candy, Fire, Galaxy, Frost, Toxic; Green is in the same list with `free: true`, open to everyone; `Player.bubble` is public so the whole table sees it, set through `setLook`, drawn by `.fx-<id>` in `premium.css`, tinted in `Bubbles.tsx`), typing any emoji as your face (the "Type any emoji" box in `EmojiPicker`; the grid stays free), and AI Legends in your games (a gold "AI Legends" section at the top of `BotPicker`, "Add an AI Legend" in the table menu and "Add legend" in the lobby; `addBot` takes an optional `legend`, `createTable` seats a legend by its short name; `LEGENDS_ON` still only controls the Home shelf and the ladder). Whatever a free player taps goes through `withPremium(reason, go)` / `openPremium()` in `store.ts`, which opens the offer (sheet `premium`, a night-purple sheet with a swaying fan of every Premium card back (`CardFan`) and legends, bubbles and emoji floating round it, three swipeable showcase cards with the one you tapped first, the price, a sticky gold button, "Restore purchase"); after buying, `then` carries on with what they were doing. `setProfile()` also refuses a Premium bubble colour or a non-grid emoji without Premium. The gold "FlipUp Premium" card sits under Settings on Profile; "Chat bubbles" is a row in Settings and the table menu (sheet `bubbles`). There is no payment network yet: `purchasePremium()` and `restorePurchase()` in `premium.ts` are stand-ins (the first always succeeds), the only two places to wire a real provider into, and `flipup.premium` on the phone is the only record. The server accepts a bubble, a card pack or a legend from any phone, so when payments exist it must check the purchase before `setLook`/`addBot` honour them. Legends are shown as their emoji on a `LEGEND_SCENES` backdrop (the drawn portraits are gone). Tests: `premium.test.ts`, `e2e/premium.spec.ts`.
- Hold'em variants (`src/game/variants.ts`, rules in `docs/RULES.md`): `settings.variant` picks Crazy Pineapple (3 hole cards, a `discard` action after the flop betting), Crazy Deuces (`wildHand()`, five of a kind on top), Ghost (a ghost hand in `hand.ghost`, hidden by `redactForDevice()` until a showdown, pots it keeps in `game.ghostPot`/`hand.carry`, pot limit via `maxRaiseTo()`) Pittsburgh (three rivers, `bestHand()` uses the best one), Die on the River (`hand.dice`), Red River (red rivers keep coming), Crucifix (a nine-card cross, `CROSS_LINES`; a hand is one line), Circle Hold'em (six cards round a clock, `CIRCLE_RING`; both hole cards plus three in a row), Memory Poker (`startHand()` first sets `game.memory`, 20 cards everyone sees for `TIMING.memoryPeek`, then deals without them; `hand.removed` is hidden by `redactForDevice()` until the hand ends; `MemoryPeek.tsx` shows them on the felt), Bean Stalk (`dealStreet()` gives everyone still in a hole card each street, `holeCountAt()`, no burns) or Imposter (`hand.imposter`, known only to that phone until the hand ends; antes; a `vote` action after the river decides the pot, `imposterShowdown()`). Each hand stores its own `variant`. `streetCards()` says how many board cards each street deals and `handSets()` lists the card sets a hand can be made from, which `bestHand()` and the bots' `variantScore()` both use; the cross and the circle are laid out by `CROSS_CELLS`/`CIRCLE_HOURS` in `Table.tsx` (`.board-cross`, `.board-circle`). Bots at a variant table play `src/game/botVariants.ts` instead of `botPlay.ts`: it plays the rest of the hand out about 150 times by the variant's rules (wild deuces, three rivers, beating the ghost...) and bets, calls or folds against the price in the bot's style; in Imposter it blends in (checks and calls, small probing bets). Nobody can fold in Imposter (`options().canFold`, `passiveMove()` for timeouts). Each variant has its own table look: `theme-<variant>` classes in `screens.css`, colours as `--theme-<variant>-*` tokens, and the variant's emoji faded into the felt. The picker is `GameRow` in `src/screens/GamePicker.tsx`; new variants go in `VARIANTS`.
- Home (`src/screens/Home.tsx`, `HomeGames.tsx`, `HomeLegends.tsx`, `src/styles/home.css`): a large title with your avatar beside it (a pen badge on it, or an empty outline with a plus before you've set up a profile; it opens your profile, where the streak row and Settings live). A slim banner row (a badge, the table, a Rejoin button) only when there's one clear next step (your open table, the online table you left, or your open party); the left table is checked against the server on load and hidden if it has closed, and it has a close button. Under it, when you have a finished game and no banner, a flat "Play <game> again" row (opens that game's start sheet). Then the two start tiles side by side with no header (Play with friends, Play with bots; a big face in the corner, `.start-face`, with a small speech bubble over it, `.start-bubble`, that pokes past the tile's top edge and says a different short line every 4s; the lines are stacked in one grid cell so the bubble never changes size, and Reduce Motion shows only the first) which open `Setup` for a plain table. Then **Games** (`GameShelf`): Texas Hold'em as one wide card and every other game in `VARIANTS` as a two-column grid tile (the game's drawn scene from `src/ui/GameIllustrations.tsx` and its name; colours from `cardArt()` in `gameThemes.ts`). Tapping a card or tile opens `GameStartSheet` (sheet `game`): the rules and Play with friends / Play with bots, which open `Setup` with that game picked. There is no "More game modes" sheet. Last, **AI Legends** (`LegendShelf`, each card the legend's emoji on their `LEGEND_SCENES` backdrop). No quick games and no History. Three tabs in a floating tab bar, Play, Party and Campaign, with a round "Join with a code" button beside it (a left rail on desktop). The daily streak counts days with a hand played (`flipup.days`) and shows in your profile, not on Home. Home never shows chip totals. There are no learning features (no GTO grading, coach, drills, Scroll, odds quizzes or AI twins). Lists elsewhere show a game as `GameThumb`, its scene in a small rounded square.
- Code entry (`src/ui/CodePad.tsx`): every 5-letter code (a table, a party, a friend) is typed with `useCode()` and `CodePad`: five boxes and a keyboard laid out like the phone's own (QWERTY rows), with the letters codes never use (vowels and Y) greyed out. The phone's keyboard never opens; a computer's keyboard types into it too. `JoinSheet` adds the QR scanner.
- Seat reactions (`MoodFace` in `table/Pods.tsx`, styles at the end of `table.css`): a seat's emoji falls over with dizzy stars when knocked out, trembles and sweats (💦) over a big bet, jumps with sparkles for a win, gets a 💔 when an all-in loses and a 🔥 on a shove, and slumps on a fold. Off with reduced motion.
- Mini game look and sound: each game's table look is Oliver's (`.table-oval.themed.theme-<game>` in `global.css` tokens and `screens.css`, plus the game's emoji faded into the felt via `data-motif`); Texas Hold'em keeps the player's felt and rail. `src/ui/gameThemes.ts` holds the rest: each game's Home card (`cardArt()`: gradient, a mood line, used by `GameShelf`, `GameStartSheet` and the game picker tiles) and its sound (`soundMoodFor()` pitches the synthesised sounds through `setSoundMood()`, low for the ghost and bright for the pineapple; `useThemeMoments()` plays the games' own stings `dice`, `spook`, `sting` and `splash`). A new game gets a `VARIANTS` entry, an `ART` entry and its table look.
- Campaign (`src/game/campaign.ts`, `roulette.ts`, `src/state/campaign.ts`, `src/screens/HomeCampaign.tsx`, `Roulette.tsx`, `CampaignResult.tsx`, `table/PrizeReel.tsx`, `sheets/CampaignIntroSheet.tsx`, `sheets/CampaignSheets.tsx`, `src/styles/campaign.css`; rules in `docs/RULES.md`): the last Home tab. A bankroll on the phone (`flipup.campaign`) that a daily salary tops up to 1,000 (only on days you open it, `collectSalary()`). Cash games set their blinds from your bankroll (`cashStakeFor()`: the biggest `STAKES` entry whose big blind is at most 1/50th of it; `CASH.fitBB`). On Home the red `CashBox` is the first card of the same shelf as the tournaments, with a divider; it opens the `cashSeat` screen (`CashSeat.tsx`: the blinds, a slider and quick picks for 20 to 100 big blinds, `startCampaign('cash', id, amount)`). `scripts/cashDepthLab.ts` plays the bots at 15 to 100 big blinds to see how deep stacks play; tournaments are `VENUES` on a sideways shelf, each one `FORMATS` entry (Sit & Go, six players, 70/30; Jackpot, three players, winner takes all) at one buy-in, opened by peak bankroll, bots from the venue's `styles`. A tournament's prize is drawn when it opens (`drawMultiplier()`, stored in `game.campaign.prize` and the seat) and `PrizeReel` shows it before the first deal; it never depends on who's at the table. Titles by peak (`TITLES`), goals with one-time chip rewards (`goals()`, `claimGoal()`, all of them in sheet `campaignGoals`) and a European roulette wheel. A campaign table has `game.campaign` set (`campaignTable()` builds its settings for the phone and the server): a cash table's busted bots are replaced at the deal (`refillCash()` in `reducer.ts`), a tournament records `game.busted` like a party round and `sngPlace()` reads your finish. Friends: "Play with friends" (sheet `campaignFriends`, `openCampaignForFriends()`) opens the table online (`POST /api/tables` with `campaign`; the server sets the stakes and draws the prize, fills seats with bots at `start`, refuses late tournament joins and `settings`); each phone pays its own buy-in when it's first seated and every online cash rebuy as `bought` grows (`payCampaign()` in `store.ts`), and the join screen shows the cost. Your bankroll, peak and wins go on your friend card (`shareCampaignSoon()` in `pals.ts`, `FriendCard.update()`), and the tab ranks you and your friends by bankroll. Chips move only through `startCampaign()`, `openCampaignForFriends()`/`payCampaign()`, `campaignRebuy()` and `settleCampaign()` in `store.ts`; `setSession()` settles the seat (`Campaign.seat`) once the table is over for you or you've left it (a lobby left or a tournament called off is refunded), and settling twice does nothing. Difficulty: each venue and stake has a `dull` (0 to 1); its bots carry `Player.dull` (stripped by `redactForDevice()`), and `playerStyle()` plays them through `dulled()` in `botStyles.ts` (more wobble, less sense, adapt and exploit). From the Riverboat up a venue has a `boss` legend: `seatBoss()` in `reducer.ts` dresses one bot seat as them (`game.campaign.boss`, at `BOSS_DULL` of the room's dullness); `game.knocked` records who put each player out, and knocking out the boss yourself pays `bossReward()` the first time (`stats.bosses`). A boss remembers you: `Campaign.bossReads` holds what that boss's table saw you do, seeded into `game.reads` next time, and `game.rematch` greets you. `prepareCampaign()` sets up a bots-only table before the confirm sheet so a bounty (`bountyChance`, `game.campaign.bounty`) can be named there; the table header shows only a small 🎯. Daily: the salary streak (`streakToday()`, `salaryFor()`), three missions (`MISSIONS`, `missionsFor(day)`, counted by `noteCampaignHand()` and other `noteMission()` calls in `store.ts`, collected with `claimMission()`), the free spin (sheet `dailySpin`, `DAILY_SPIN`, `takeFreeSpin()`). Weekly winnings (`Campaign.week`, `tally()`) and a brag go on the friend card for the "This week" board. Winning a venue gives its card back (`CARD_PACKS` with `venue`, `packOpen()` in `store.ts`). Roulette is capped by `rouletteLimit()`. `scripts/campaignLab.ts` plays real campaign tournaments with a scripted person and then whole climbs from 1,000 chips; results in `docs/CAMPAIGN.md`. The first visit opens the intro sheet (`Campaign.intro`). `homeView` in `store.ts` remembers the Home tab. Campaign tables never show bots' cards: `revealBots` is refused and `showToBusted` peeking is off when `game.campaign` is set. A win on `CampaignResult` flies chips from the prize into the bankroll (`flyChips()` in `effects.ts`) while the number counts up. Tests: `campaign.test.ts`, `e2e/campaign.spec.ts`.
- Floor games and Blackjack (`src/game/blackjack.ts`, `worker/blackjack.ts`, `src/state/blackjack.ts`, `src/screens/Blackjack.tsx`, `sheets/BlackjackSheets.tsx`, `src/styles/blackjack.css`; rules in `docs/RULES.md`): the Campaign tab's "Floor games" section holds the roulette card and the Blackjack card (sheet `bjStart`, then the `blackjack` screen). Vincent 🤵🏻‍♂️ (`DEALER`) deals; a smooth Vegas charmer whose lines are `DEALER_LINES` (the bots' are `BOT_LINES`), stored in `game.talk` with the time they're said and shown over the speaker. Every table seats one or two random bots (`pickBjBots()`, tempers `book`/`careful`/`wild` on top of `basicMove()`). `applyBj()` is the only way a table changes (internal `tick` and `presence`, never from a phone; `parseBjAction()`), `redactBj()` hides the shoe and the hole card. On your own the table runs on the phone (`flipup.blackjack`, its own clock); with friends it's a `BlackjackRoom` Durable Object (`POST /api/blackjack`, socket `/api/blackjack/:code/ws`). Friends come only through invites from your friends list: `POST /api/friends/:code/invite` puts a `BjInvite` on their `FriendCard` (named from the inviter's own card, which their phone must own), and every phone with a friend code checks `GET /api/friends/:code/invites` every 15s (`watchInvites()`), showing `BjInviteBanner` over any screen. Chips: bets come from the campaign bankroll; `bjSettle()` in `campaign.ts` takes the stake as the cards are dealt (and a double or split) and pays from `game.history` once per round (`Campaign.bj` is the ledger), so the server never holds chips. Tests: `blackjack.test.ts`, `e2e/blackjack.spec.ts`.
- Party tournaments (`src/game/party.ts`, `worker/party.ts`, `src/screens/Party.tsx`, `PartySetup.tsx`, `HomeParty.tsx`): the Party tab replaced the old Review tab. A host picks 4–12 mini games (any of the variants, repeats allowed; `PartySettings`), chips per game, how often the blinds double (`blindHands` in `Settings`, counted in hands so rounds are quick and don't depend on a clock) and how far bots fill the table. Friends join by a 5-letter party code (`PartyRoom` Durable Object, `/api/parties`, polled every ~2s by `usePartyView()`; lobby only, no late joins). Each round is a normal online `TableRoom` that `buildPartyGame()` seats everyone at (no rebuys, 20s turns, `game.party` says which round) and starts; busted players are noted in order (`game.busted`, `noteBusts()` in `reducer.ts`). The table reports chips to the party after every hand (`reportToParty()` in `room.ts`, so the leaderboard moves live) and its finish order when it ends; places among the *people* score `PLACE_POINTS` (bots never score). The party then waits `PARTY_BREAK_SECONDS` (or the host skips) and opens the next table; phones follow it with `useFollowTable()` (`joinTable()`). A party table's finish screen is the `PartyPanel` leaderboard instead of `Final`, and its header has a round pill that opens the party screen. `partyStandings()` is pure: ranks, moves since the last round, provisional points for the running round (the board rows slide to their rank). Tests: `party.test.ts`, `e2e/party.spec.ts`.
- Friends (`src/state/pals.ts`, `worker/friendCard.ts`, `/api/friends`, Party tab): each phone claims a 5-letter friend code (a name and picture in a `FriendCard` Durable Object, owned by the device id, never returned); you add friends by typing their code (`AddPalSheet` in `sheets/CodeSheets.tsx`) or with "+ Friend" on a party leaderboard row (members show their `friendCode` once the server checks the phone owns it). The list is on the phone (`flipup.pals`). Separate from Friends' AI twins (`friends.ts`).
- Join popup (`src/screens/sheets/JoinSheet.tsx`, scanner in `src/ui/QRScanner.tsx`): "Join with a code" opens a sheet with five code boxes (the keyboard only opens when you tap them) and a "Scan a QR code" button. The scanner uses `BarcodeDetector` where the browser has it and `jsqr` otherwise. A join link (`/ABCDE`) still opens the small `Join` screen in `Join.tsx`.
- Picking bots (`src/screens/Setup.tsx`, `BotPicker.tsx`): on the bots-only setup, "Who plays" is either Random table (recommended, the bot-count stepper) or Pick your bots, which opens `BotPicker`: every bot in `BOTS`, grouped by playstyle (ten groups, with a line on how each plays: this is the one place styles are shown), a name search, jump chips and a count out of eight; Save returns the names to Setup, and `openTable('local', settings, n, names)` seats exactly them. This screen's copy of each style's blurb is `GROUPS` in `BotPicker.tsx`.
- The table screen: `src/screens/Table.tsx` is the frame (header, felt, timer, win banner); `table/Pods.tsx` is the other players, your box and the "+N" list, `table/Bubbles.tsx` is speech bubbles and quick messages, `table/Actions.tsx` is the action row, pre-actions and the raise panel.
- `src/state/store.ts`: the app store and every screen command. Bots-only tables run `applyAction()` locally with their own clock; online tables send actions to the server and show what it sends back.
- `src/ui/sound.ts` (synthesised table sounds, plus four mp3s in `public/sounds`) and `src/ui/useTableSounds.ts` (plays them by watching how the game changes). `Count` in `components.tsx` is the counting-number animation for pot and stacks.
- Per-phone settings live in `src/state/persist.ts`: table felt/rail (`flipup.table`), Appearance (`flipup.appearance`: auto follows the phone, or light, or dark), Transparency (`flipup.glass`), mute, lifetime totals and the last online table for Home. Settings (Home → your avatar → Settings, and the "You" part of the table menu) is one grouped list with iPhone switches (`Rows` in `SheetHost.tsx`). Haptics (`haptic()` in `src/ui/effects.ts`) use `navigator.vibrate` on Android and, on iPhone, flip a hidden switch-style checkbox, which Safari 18+ answers with a Taptic tick; buttons show a press by shrinking and dimming (no ripples). Pre-actions (Check/Fold, Call current, Call any) are screen state in `Table.tsx`, never sent to the server until your turn. Off your turn they fill the same three slots as Fold, Check and Raise; Call current clears itself if the bet changes.
- Desktop: `src/styles/desktop.css` is one media query (min-width 960px and min-height 600px, the same as `useDesktop` in `src/ui/useDesktop.ts`), so phones never see it. `installDesktopScale()` sets `--desk-zoom` from a 1440×900 baseline; the table is `zoom`ed by it. On desktop the table has a `.stage` (players round the oval, positions from `--ax`/`--ay` set in `Table.tsx`) and a side panel (`SidePanel.tsx`: standings and live hand log). On a phone `.stage` is `display: contents` and the row of four slides. Keep phone CSS outside that media query.
- VS intro (`src/screens/table/Versus.tsx`, styles at the end of `table.css`): a heads-up game (two players: a bot, a legend or a friend) opens with a Street Fighter style versus screen in place of the countdown: a blue panel (you, top) and a red one (them, bottom; a legend keeps their own `LEGEND_SCENES` colours) split by a diagonal slash, the fighters flying in from either side, VS slamming in with a shake, name plates, and rays from the middle of the screen to every corner of each panel (`Rays` draws triangles to points round the panel edge, corners included; a bright flash sweeps from ray to ray round each fan, `vs-shimmer`, and VS pulses after it lands; thin speed lines keep flying across each panel, `vs-line`). No flash, no blinds, no extra emoji. `VersusGate` in `Table.tsx` shows it while `!hand && nextHandAt`; it clears itself after `TIMING.versusDelay` (2.6s; the CSS delays are timed to it) and a tap skips it. The game waits on it: `greetWait()` in `holdem.ts` puts the bot's hello `versusHello` after the intro (a fast table's clock runs twice as fast, so twice as long in table time), `start` pencils the deal in just after (`startWait()`), and when the hello is said `reducer.ts` moves `nextHandAt` to after it has been said and read plus `TIMING.afterHello`, so no cards come while the bot is talking. Heads-up has no "Dealing in" countdown. Reduced motion gets the finished picture with a fade.
- Any other new game waits `TIMING.startDelay` (3s) after Deal before the first hand (`start` in `reducer.ts` sets `nextHandAt`; the table shows "Dealing in 3…"). Inside it a bot greets through `pendingTalk`: `hello` heads-up, naming the person (lines hold `{you}`, `reactionText(id, name)` fills it, and `reactions[id].to` says who), `welcome` for a bigger table.
- Reacting faces (`moodOf()` and `MoodFace` in `table/Pods.tsx`, styles before the table cam's in `table.css`): a seat's emoji reacts to the hand from public moves only: a bounce for a win, a shake when an all-in loses at showdown, a droop (greyed) when knocked out, a pop for a shove, a sink on a fold, and a sweat drop while it thinks over a big bet (half the pot or 40% of its stack and at least three big blinds, or an all-in). Keyed on hand and mood so each reaction plays from the start.
- With more than four others the row is a window; the "+N" badge (`HiddenPlayers` in `table/Pods.tsx`) opens a small list of the players out of it with their chips.
- Play again (`playAgain` action, `openAgain()` in `reducer.ts`, buttons in `Final.tsx`): on a finished table the host's tap turns it into a new lobby (new `game.id`, the blinds and bot count from `game.first`, fresh stacks); a friend's tap only adds them to `game.again`, and only those people are seated when the host opens it. Anyone not seated sees "A new game is open" with a Sit down button (`Lobby.tsx`). Names are unique per table: a taken one gets " 2", " 3"… (`uniqueName()`), within the 10 character limit. `useWakeLock()` keeps the screen on while a table is going.
- Loading screen: `index.html` draws its own splash (`#splash`: three cards flipping, the name, "Slow connection. Still dealing…" after 6s and a Try again button after 20s) from inline styles and a tiny inline script that applies the saved appearance, so a slow first load never shows a blank page; `main.tsx` fades it out once the app has drawn. Its colours copy the `global.css` tokens, since no stylesheet has loaded yet. Tests: `e2e/splash.spec.ts`.
- Offline start: `public/sw.js` (registered in `src/main.tsx`, production only) keeps the app so Home and bot games open with no connection. Pages are network first (a new deploy arrives at once, but a page slower than 2.5s gives way to the saved copy), files under `/assets`, `/sounds` and `/icons` are cached on first use, `/api` is never touched, and an HTML answer is never stored as a script (the single-page fallback returns one for missing files). Bump `VERSION` in it if its behaviour changes. `e2e/offline.spec.ts` builds the app and runs it with `vite preview`.
- A dropped phone: the table room (`worker/room.ts`) tells the game which devices are connected with the internal `presence` action (never accepted from a phone, like `tick`). A person who isn't connected gets `player.offline`, shown as an "Offline" tag on their seat, and when the table has a clock their turn is capped at `TIMING.offlineClock` (15s). A table with no clock is left alone.
- The hand log's share button (`handLogText()` in `src/game/handText.ts`) sends a finished hand as plain text through the phone's share sheet, or copies it.
- Quick messages are `react` actions from the fixed list in `src/game/reactions.ts`; nothing typed is ever accepted. Table talk (`src/game/chat.ts`, `src/screens/table/Bubbles.tsx`, `botLines.ts`): a bot's line is one bubble above the seat, said the way a game character says it. After a moment of typing dots the words come through the bubble a few at a time (`ticker()` and `windowAt()` in `chat.ts`: at most three words and about 15 letters on show, about 15 letters a second, a little longer at a comma and longer at a full stop, sped up so no line takes over 3.4s). Each new word slides in at the right while the oldest fades and its place closes up, the last words hold for 0.8s, then the bubble fades. `Ticker` in `Bubbles.tsx` draws it, and the bubble is sized by an invisible copy of the widest window (`widestWindow()`), so it never changes size or reaches into a neighbour. `|` and `||` in a line's text are read as spaces; a person's quick message is shown whole. `ChatLane` is the sequencer (pure, takes the time as an argument): one speaker types at a time, the next may start `replyGap` after the last message lands (so bots answer each other), a person's quick message goes up at once, and lines that went stale (a "c'mon" after the card, a remark about a move already made) are dropped. `botSays()` returns how long a line takes to say and read, a bot waits that long (`game.talkDone`) before it moves, and the next deal waits for the talk. Bots answer a person's quick message (`thanks`, `ribbed`, `goodLuck`, `gg`), each other (`cheer`, `commiserate`) and say hi back (`greetBack`); a legend with a signature hand says so when it wins with it (`signature`). Line ids are indexes and only ever grow at the end (`botLines.ts` adds the second pass). `scripts/chatLab.ts` plays tables and measures how the talk would feel; `e2e/chat-timing.spec.ts` measures it in a real browser.
- Legends (hidden for launch: `LEGENDS_ON = false` in `legends.ts` removes the Home shelf, the finish screen's next-legend card and `startLegend()`; the code and tests stay) (`src/game/legends.ts`, `legendTalk.ts`, plan in `docs/LEGENDS.md`): bots in the style of famous players (real names, emoji faces, no photos, original lines, and `LEGEND_NOTE` wherever one is named). A legend is a set of habit numbers fitted to a `Style` by `fitStyle()` (in `twinFit.ts`, left over from the AI twins, which are gone) with habits laid over it; `dressAsLegend()` puts one in a bot seat (`Player.legend` is public, its brain in `Player.twin` is stripped by `redactForDevice()`), and `botSays()` speaks in its own voice. `scripts/twinLab.ts legends` checks them against their numbers and ranks them. On Play, `HomeLegends.tsx` shows the shelf (backdrops in `LEGEND_SCENES`, `botScenes.ts`), `Legend.tsx` is a profile with what they're known for and a Play button (`startLegend()`, `Game.legendGame` so Play again sits you down again), and a legend's seat has a gold star chip. Locked in ladder order: the first is open and each next one opens when you knock the one before out heads-up (`legendOpen()` in `state/progress.ts`; `startLegend()` refuses a locked one). A legend greets you differently when you've met (`Game.rematch`, events `rematchWon`/`rematchLost`) and says a closing line on the finish screen (`finishLine()` in `botTalk.ts`, events `finishWon`/`finishLost`).
- Progress and the finish screen (`src/state/progress.ts`, `src/train/tableStats.ts`, `src/screens/Final.tsx`): `flipup.progress` keeps legends beaten, badges (`BADGES`, shown in History), the lifetime counters badges read and your record against each legend. A finished table adds a line to `flipup.stats.games` with its `variant`, which History shows. `tableStats()` is pure: from the saved `HandRecord`s of one table it gives hands won, biggest pot, bluffs that worked (won with nothing and no showdown), showdowns, all-ins, best hand, a chips-after-each-hand series and a style word. `settleTable()` counts a table once (keyed by game id, the report is kept so the page can be reopened) and returns what it earned: badges, the legend it opened, a comeback, the series score. `Final` calls it on mount and is one long scrolling page with no suspense: a big result, announcements, huge stat tiles that count up as they scroll into view, the chips line, your style, the next legend and the standings. The share button sends a picture drawn on a canvas (`ui/shareCard.ts`), or text where the phone can't share files. "Fast bot games" in Settings (`flipup.fast`) sets `Game.fast` when a bots-only table is dealt: its clock runs at twice the speed (`state/clock.ts`).
- `worker/room.ts`: one `TableRoom` Durable Object per join code, the only true copy of an online table and its deck. Its alarm is the table's clock (bots, turn timers, run-outs, next deal) and also deletes tables idle for 3 days.

- Bot identity: bots never wear a face from the profile picker (`botFace()` in `botProfiles.ts` swaps any picker emoji for an animal or object nobody can pick), and every bot seat carries a small BOT chip. Speech bubbles are plain capsules with no tail. Joining is the one round button beside the tab bar (a keypad icon, `KeypadIcon`); Home has no separate join bar.

- Motion: `navigate()`/`back()` in `store.ts` wrap the screen change in a View Transition (`slide()`, `html[data-nav]`, keyframes at the end of `global.css`): the new screen slides in over the old one, back is the reverse, and browsers without it (or Reduce Motion on a touch phone) keep the old fade. Sheets rise over 0.38s on the iOS curve. The code pad (`CodePad.tsx`) blinks a cursor, shakes red on a wrong code and ripples while it looks the code up. Seats show the chips they've put in this round as a small tag. Game cards (`GameIllustrations.tsx`) are drawn from real playing cards in the four-colour deck, one layout per game.

## Rules that matter

- Hidden information stays hidden. `redactForDevice()` strips the deck and every hole card that isn't yours or face up. Bots-only tables keep the deck on the phone but the screens still only get the redacted view.
- Win/tie odds show only during an all-in run-out, when every live hand is face up. Never while betting is still possible (product owner's call).
- Every timed thing goes through `game.wakeAt` and a `tick` action. `parseAction()` never accepts `tick` from a phone.
- Chips are never created or lost: the "keeps every chip accounted for" test plays bot hands and checks the total after every step. Keep it passing.
- When a poker rule is unclear, ask the product owner, then write the answer in `docs/RULES.md`.

## UI style

Apple-like, with a party streak (every menu: setup, lobby, party, profile, sheets and the finish page; the game screen once cards are dealt keeps its own sizes, see the end of `global.css`): it should feel like Apple made a poker app for a night with friends, so bolder and bigger. Loud gradients on what you tap to play, chunky buttons with a bottom edge (`--party-*` tokens in `global.css`), heavy (800) large titles and the main tap targets, bold (700) card headers, big tap targets, a bright capsule on the chosen tab and a hot-pink join button. The Party block at the end of `home.css` holds the Home version. The Party tab has one loud card (Host a party), then flat rows (Your party, Join a party, Add a friend), a flat friend code cell and a bold "Friends" heading. No ambient glow behind screens or cards: colour lives in the gradient on what you tap, with a soft shadow (`--shadow-soft`) under it. System font only (SF Pro on iPhone, the platform default elsewhere), regular weight for body text, semibold for headings and buttons, bold for large titles and for the headers of cards and buttons you tap (Play with Friends, the game cards, banners). Light mode is white with `#f2f2f7` cells, dark mode is true black with `#1c1c1e` cells, and the accent is system blue. Cells are flat (a soft fill, no outline, no shadow). Glass (the blurred, see-through look) stays on what floats: the tab bar, capsules, sheets and toasts. Large titles on Home, grouped rows with a coloured icon square and a chevron, a banner card with its own action bar for the one main next step. Instant feedback (press scale, haptics, confetti, card flips) that never blocks play. Profile pictures are emojis (pick from the grid or type any). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Plain writing: no buzzwords, contractions are fine.

Every colour, size, radius and shadow is a token in `:root` in `src/styles/global.css`. Colours needed from code live in `src/ui/theme.ts`. Don't hard-code new values; spacing is on a 4px grid.
