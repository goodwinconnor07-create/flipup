# FlipUp audit: Human Interface Guidelines and App Review Guidelines

16 November 2026, on branch `claude/hig-review-audit` from `main` at `fd4ba48`.

## Read this first: how far the audit got

The plan was eight reviewers, one per area, each reading every file in its area. All eight were cut off by the
account's usage limit before they wrote anything, and the owner then asked for the rest to be kept lean. So this
audit is narrower than the plan:

- **Every control in the app was checked by machine** for the things a machine can check: 286 controls, alerts and
  fields listed from the code itself (the table at the end), every pressable's role and label, every text field's
  settings, every alert's wording and button styles, every font size, every timer and loop, every string a person
  can see (other platforms, unfinished wording, money language), and every development switch.
- **The highest-risk screens were read by hand**: the Premium page and the purchase code, How to Play, Settings'
  restore, share, rate and delete paths, the daily reminder, the camera's refusal path, the player menu, the root
  stack and tab bar, the launch cover's settings, the privacy page, What's New, and the parts of the table, felt,
  seats, playlist, trainer and replay player that the fixes touch.
- **The layout was measured** in the web export with `scripts/layoutSweep.mjs`. Its first run found the web build
  stopping at launch, a real bug that is fixed here (fix 0); the second run's findings are under "The layout sweep".
- **Not done**: a line-by-line read of the other screens. The inventory says which is which: "read", "swept" (the
  machine checks only) or "measured". Nothing was seen on an iPhone, so every pass below is "code-checked".

## The checklist

Each rule has an id used in the tables below.

**A. Accessibility** (HIG Accessibility, VoiceOver, Typography, Color, Dark Mode)
A1 every pressable has a role and a label that says what it does · A2 decoration is hidden from VoiceOver · A3
pictures, cards and charts have a text alternative · A4 groups read as one sentence, headings are marked · A5 a
change that happens by itself is announced · A6 touch targets 44 x 44pt (28pt only for minor controls) · A7 text
contrast 4.5:1 up to 17pt, 3:1 from 18pt or bold · A8 no text under 11pt · A9 Dynamic Type: text scales and layouts
reflow · A10 colour is never the only signal · A11 Reduce Motion · A12 Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast ·
A13 nothing on a timer is the only path, least of all under VoiceOver · A14 no flashing; loops stop off screen · A15
gestures have a button or action alternative · A16 haptics and sound come with something to see.

**B. Layout and navigation** (HIG Layout, Tab bars, Sheets, Modality, Launching)
B1 safe areas · B2 a way back and a way out of every screen · B3 the tab bar only navigates and is never hidden on
tab roots · B4 one sheet at a time, a grabber, swipe to dismiss · B5 no screen titled with the app's name · B6
fields: right keyboard, Return key, hint, stays above the keyboard · B7 no scroll inside scroll · B9 a plain launch
screen with no words · B10 size classes, iPad · B13 empty, loading and error states.

**C. Controls** (HIG Buttons, Alerts, Action sheets, Menus, Toggles, Sliders, Lists, Progress, Page controls)
C1 system controls unless there's a reason · C2 one prominent button, never the destructive one · C3 alerts: only
when it matters, a short title, verbs on buttons, Cancel beside a red destructive button · C4 choices after a tap are
an action sheet, not an alert · C5 switches only in rows · C7 sliders are adjustable for VoiceOver · C9 progress
shown in place · C10 page dots, no auto-advance that can't be stopped · C11 sharing through the system share sheet
· C12 nothing only in a long-press menu · C13 SF Symbols with their usual meaning, no emoji as icons · C14 undo
over confirm.

**D. Writing** (HIG Writing, Inclusion)
D1 plain, consistent words · D2 errors say what happened and what to do · D5 no placeholder, debug or "coming
soon" text.

**E. Motion, haptics, audio** (HIG Motion, Playing haptics, Playing audio; `docs/MOTION.md`)
E1 motion has a purpose · E2 haptics by their meaning, and optional · E3 sound follows the silent switch · E4 the 20
rules hold for anything touched.

**F. Privacy** (HIG Privacy; App Review 5.1.1, 5.1.2, 2.5.14)
F1 permission asked only when the feature is used, with a clear purpose string and a way forward when refused · F3
no tracking, ads or analytics · F4 the privacy manifest, the App Privacy answers and the policy match what's really
sent and kept · F5 privacy policy and support in the app · F6 data deletion in the app.

**G. Purchases** (HIG In-app purchase; App Review 3.1.1, 3.1.2, 2.1(b), 5.6)
G1 only StoreKit unlocks things · G2 each plan shows its name, length, full price from StoreKit, and an intro
offer's price, length and the price after it · G3 renewal terms, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy beside the button
· G4 Restore on the page and in Settings, Manage subscription, the renewal date · G5 loading, failed, pending,
cancelled and can't-pay states · G6 no dark patterns · G7 chips can't be bought.

**H. Safety and content** (App Review 1.1, 1.2, 4.5.6, 5.2, 5.3)
H1 names, emoji and quick messages: filter, Report, Block, contact · H2 simulated gambling only, said in the app ·
H3 bot lines clean · H4 third-party names and content used with the right to · H5 Apple emoji only as text.

**I. Completeness** (App Review 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.5)
I1 nothing unfinished, hidden or switched off · I2 every link resolves · I3 no other platforms mentioned · I4 no
downloaded code · I7 the system rating prompt, after real use · I8 notifications optional and asked in context ·
I9 the listing text in the repo matches the build.

## What couldn't be verified without a device

- Anything VoiceOver actually says, and its reading order on the table. The labels, roles and the new
  announcements are in the code; none has been heard.
- Dynamic Type at the accessibility sizes, on any screen. `ThemedText` scales to 3.1x on scrolling pages and the
  table is capped on purpose; whether a page clips at AX5 needs a phone.
- Contrast on the moving light fields at their brightest. CLAUDE.md records the measurement behind the 80% and 66%
  label colours; it wasn't repeated.
- Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Bold Text as drawn.
- The purchase flow: StoreKit prices, the $1 first month's wording from real products, Ask to Buy, Restore.
- Safe areas and the Dynamic Island, the keyboard over the code and name fields, the camera's permission prompt
  and its refusal, the notification prompt, Save Image.
- Haptics and sound under the silent switch.
- The app on an iPad in iPhone mode.
- The built `.ipa`'s privacy manifest against the required-reason APIs its packages use.
- That every link answers (privacy, support, Apple's EULA, the 210 source links in Range training, 22 of them to YouTube).
