# How to Play: the script

Each game's How to Play is one real hand on the iPhone app's own table, shot on an iPhone simulator. Every step is
a moment of that hand, zoomed in on what it's about, with a short title and one line. The rules for writing them:

- **Show, don't explain.** The picture does the work. The line says what you're looking at and why it's fun.
- **Lead with the twist.** A mini game gets to the thing that makes it different by step two or three. Hold'em
  basics are said once, in Texas Hold'em, not repeated in every game.
- **Short.** A title is one to four words. A line is one or two short sentences, under about 16 words.
- **True.** Every line matches `docs/RULES.md` and what's in the picture.

Zooms: **mine** (your cards and box), **lower** (the bottom half), **hand** (your cards, box and buttons), **seats** (the players along the top), **board** (the felt),
**big board** (Pittsburgh's rivers, Red River's long board), **round** (the cross, the circle and Memory's twenty, cropped sideways too), **full** (the whole screen), **showdown** (the seats, the board and who won).

## Texas Hold'em

| # | Moment | Zoom | Title | Line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deal | mine | Two cards, all yours | Nobody else sees them. Poker face on. |
| 2 | Your turn | seats | Read the table | Everyone checks, bets, calls, raises or folds in turn. Every move tells you something. |
| 3 | Your turn | hand | Your move | Fold and wait for the next one, call to stay in, or raise and make them sweat. |
| 4 | Flop | board | The flop | Three cards everyone shares. Now hands start to take shape. |
| 5 | Turn | board | The turn | Card number four. Pots tend to grow from here. |
| 6 | River | board | The river | The last card and the last bet. Bluff, or get paid. |
| 7 | Showdown | showdown | Showdown | Cards up. Best five from your two and the board takes it all. |
