# Range training: where every hand comes from

Every hand in `src/train/ranges/scenarios.ts` is a hand from a published breakdown (a real hand, or a teaching hand the expert built to make a point): the article's seats, action, board, cards and answer, with the opponent's range built from the expert's own read. This file lists them so nobody adds a hand twice and every answer can be checked against its source.

Rules (the owner's, November 2026):

- Only hands from published breakdowns (strategy articles, coaching quizzes, hand reviews, readable video summaries). Never invent a hand.
- Never change the expert's answer. If the trainer's numbers disagree, fix the ranges to match what the article says, or drop the hand.
- Skip hands whose verdict is only in a video nobody could read (a video works when its captions and paused frames give every card, size and the expert’s words), hands with missing cards, heads-up games, limit games, and spots the trainer can't represent fairly: implied odds on a draw, plans that span two streets (check now to win more on the river), check-raise plans, or a player still to act behind you when you face a bet.
- When the article leaves out sizes, stacks or seats, use standard ones and say so in `source.note`.
