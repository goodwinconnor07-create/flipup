This pass implements elementary engineering adaptations; it does not reproduce these systems or independently verify their experiments. Full-paper review, including the supplied February 2026 MIT thesis, remains outstanding. No trained human policy, equilibrium guarantee, or human indistinguishability claim is made.

## Approximate strategy

The fallback constructs exact two-card combinations excluding visible blockers. Public aggressive/calling/checking actions multiply smoothed untrained likelihoods derived from starting-hand quality. It does not infer a perfectly optimal opponent. Sequential conditional opponent sampling removes previously assigned cards, then samples an unknown board independently of the real deck. This introduces joint-range approximation and seat-order dependence; tests establish card validity, not posterior accuracy.

Equity is expected showdown pot share with a sample standard error. Approximate action values now reuse production pot construction on hypothetical deals, cap calls at available stacks, return unmatched chips, and estimate continuation from hypothetical current hand strength and price. Future betting, re-raises and position-dependent realization remain omitted; continuation likelihoods are untrained. Ties use fractional expected chip shares. They are not Nash values, solver EVs or a guarantee of strong play. Softmax temperature and preference bounds are engineering defaults, not fitted optimal percentages. The provider returns its source, version and warnings on every decision.

## Tooling decisions

Keep the repository evaluator and rules as the executable reference. OpenSpiel is appropriate for later Kuhn/Leduc exploitability experiments, but adds no validated production capability in this slice. PokerKit could independently cross-check rules in a later validation task; it is not installed or substituted for the existing engine. Their current licences and APIs need review before adoption. The C++ solver remains the candidate offline strategy producer, not an expensive solve in every request.

## Tests still needed

Larger replicated paired experiments beyond the implemented scripted population suite; confidence calibration against ground-truth changing strategies; position/board-aware behaviour; multiway strength; broader diversity calibration beyond the implemented 32-context synthetic rejection battery and calibrated profile weaknesses; independent rules differential testing; full-paper review and commercial data rights. A future human study should use consenting participants, blind anonymized histories, preregistered metrics and confidence intervals, separately from poker profitability.
