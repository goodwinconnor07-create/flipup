# The parked games' rules

Cut from `docs/RULES.md` when these eight games were taken out of FlipUp (16 November 2026). Put them back in its
"Hold'em variants" section with the games.

- **Secret Missions.** Dealt and bet like Texas Hold'em, plus missions (`src/game/missions.ts`, `src/game/missionList.ts`). Everyone dealt in holds one secret mission, a goal for a hand. Phones see only their own mission; everyone's progress in the race is public. A mission is checked at the end of every hand from what the hand recorded. When it's completed, every other player dealt in that hand (folded ones too) pays the player a reward in big blinds by the mission's difficulty: **5** for easy, **7** for medium, **10** for hard (product owner's call, November 2026), or what they have left, after the pots are paid. Several players can complete theirs in one hand; they're paid in seat order. A completed mission is replaced at the next deal. One that isn't completed is kept, hand after hand, until it is; a mission held for **15 hands** without being completed is swapped for a different one at the deal. The missions:
  - Easy (5): **🃏 Small Ball**, win a pot with one pair or less (the best hand from your cards and the board as it ended); **🤫 Silent Winner**, win a pot after the flop without a showdown; **🎨 Suited Up**, win holding two cards of one suit; **👯 Twins**, win holding a pocket pair; **❤️ Seeing Red**, win holding two red cards; **👑 Royalty**, win holding a jack, queen or king; **🔘 Button Boss**, win a pot from the button; **🛡️ Blind Defender**, win a pot from the big blind; **🥷 Blind Steal**, raise (or move all in) before the flop and win before any board card comes; **🌊 River Rat**, win a pot that reached the river.
  - Medium (7): **🐌 Slow Roll**, win a pot without betting, raising or moving all in; **👀 Hero Call**, your last move on the river is a call and you win at the showdown; **🎯 Pressure**, someone folds on the river after your last bet or raise there (winning isn't needed); **7️⃣ Lucky Seven**, win a pot holding a 7; **🏔️ Big Game**, win 25 big blinds or more from the pots; **🐣 Underdog**, win with no card higher than a 9; **🔗 Connected**, win holding two cards next to each other in rank (A-2 counts); **✌️ Two Pair Party**, win with two pair; **🪤 Trap Door**, check and then raise (or move all in) on the same street after the flop (winning isn't needed); **💥 Shove and Win**, go all in and win a pot; **🎪 Crowd Pleaser**, win a pot that four or more players saw the flop of; **2️⃣ Deuce Is Loose**, win holding a 2; **😤 Bully**, bet, raise or move all in three times in one hand (winning isn't needed).
  - Hard (10): **💪 Relentless**, bet, raise or move all in on every street you play (two or more streets) and win; **🌈 Monster**, win at the showdown with a straight or better; **🔥 Knockout**, win a pot that a player who started the hand with chips was in, who ends it with none; **🎭 Bluff Master**, win a pot with high card; **🤝 Chop Chop**, split a pot with another player.
  **The race.** Completing missions counts towards a race to **5**. Players reach it in order, and once **half the table** (rounded up; everyone at the table who has held a mission) has reached it, the race is settled at the end of that hand: the players who finished keep their order, the rest are placed by missions completed, then by chips. Each place changes that player's stack: **1st +150%, 2nd +100%, 3rd +50%, 4th +25%, 5th no change, 6th −25%, 7th −50%, 8th and 9th −75%** (rounded to whole chips). **This is the one place in FlipUp where chips are created or removed** (product owner's call, November 2026): the race's changes don't come from the other players. Then everyone's count goes back to zero, everyone gets a new mission at the next deal, and a new race starts. The table waits 2 seconds longer after a hand with a completed mission, and 3.5 seconds more when a race is settled, so the banners can be read. Bots hold missions too and go after their own: a bot with a mission about its starting cards (Twins, Suited Up, Seeing Red, Royalty, Lucky Seven, Deuce Is Loose, Connected, Underdog) plays more of the hands that fit it, Slow Roll only checks and calls, Trap Door checks a good hand to raise, Shove and Win shoves a good hand, Relentless and Bully bet every street, Pressure bets the river, Hero Call calls it, and a bot only ever reads its own mission.

- **Slide Switch.** Dealt and bet like Texas Hold'em (`src/game/slide.ts`; product owner's design, November 2026). As soon as the flop lands, before any flop betting, everyone still in the hand (all in included) picks one of their two cards and slides it face down to the next player still in on their left; at the same moment they get one from the next player still in on their right, and it takes the place of the card that left. Everyone picks at once and in secret. The same happens when the turn lands and when the river lands, so there are three slides in every hand that reaches the river, each before that street's betting (in an all-in run-out too). Heads-up the two players swap a card. Folded players are skipped. With a clock, anyone who hasn't picked when it runs out slides the card that helps them least with the board (on the flop: the card that pairs the board, shares its suits or sits near its ranks is kept; on the turn and river: the card that makes the better five-card hand with the board is kept); without a clock the table waits. A slid card is only ever known to the two players it passed between; the table sees only that everyone slid. Bots slide the card that helps them least, about one time in eight a card at random. No run-out odds are shown in this game, because the slides still to come change everyone's hand.

- **Headband.** Dealt and bet like Texas Hold'em (`src/game/twists.ts`; product owner's pick, November 2026). Each player's second card is their headband card: every other phone sees it face up (`hand.headbands`, filled in by `redactForDevice()`), and its owner's phone is never sent it until the hand is over or their cards turn face up (an all-in run-out turns everyone's over, theirs included). Hands are scored on both cards as normal. Bots play fair: they don't use their own headband card, they see everyone else's, and they guess theirs in their run-outs.
- **Conveyor Belt.** Four hole cards each. The board is a belt of three: the flop puts three cards on it; on the turn, the river and the last stop (a second river round with its own betting, `hand.lastStop`), the leftmost card falls off (`hand.dropped`, face up for everyone) and a new card comes in on the right. A hand is the best five of the player's four cards and the three on the belt at the end. No run-out odds.
- **Onion.** Five hole cards each, no burn cards (nine players need 45 cards plus the board). After the flop betting and after the turn betting, everyone still in (all in included) peels one card: everyone picks at once in secret, then the peeled cards turn over together, face up in front of each player (`hand.peeled`). When the river lands, everyone peels once more before the river betting, so the river is played with two cards. With a clock, anyone who hasn't picked peels the card whose loss hurts their hand least; without one the table waits. An all-in run-out stops for each peel. Folded players' cards stay hidden.
- **Chain Reaction.** Every board card after the first must share a rank or a suit with the board card before it. A card that doesn't link is burned face up beside the board (`hand.burned`) and the next card is tried; no other burn cards. If the deck runs that low, the next card is taken anyway.
- **Elevator.** When the turn is dealt, the three flop cards first each go up one rank (2 to 3 ... K to A, A to 2, same suit); when the river is dealt, the four cards already out go up again. The new card lands as it comes, hole cards never change. A new board card that would match a card the climbing board already shows is passed over, so the board never shows the same card twice; it can show a card someone holds, and both count.
- **Double Board PLO.** Pot Limit Omaha with five hole cards (four each when nine players are dealt in) and two boards, no burn cards. Both boards' flops come together, then both turns, then both rivers (`PLO_BOARDS` in `variants.ts`). On each board a hand is exactly two hole cards and exactly three of that board's cards (`ploBest()`). Every pot, main and side, is split in half (the odd chip to board one): each half goes to the best hand on its board among the players in that pot. Bets are pot limit, as in Haunted Pot. No run-out odds.
