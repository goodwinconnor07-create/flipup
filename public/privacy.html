Privacy policy

FlipUp for iPhone · Last updated 9 November 2026

FlipUp is poker night on your phones: no-limit Texas Hold'em and a set of mini games, played for play chips with friends or against bots. This page says what FlipUp keeps, why, for how long, who helps us run it, and how to delete it.

In short. There are no accounts, no logins, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your poker record and settings stay on your phone. What reaches our server is only what an online table or party needs to run, and that is deleted automatically after a few days. The few things we keep longer are listed below.

Play chips only

Chips in FlipUp are play chips. They have no cash value and can't be bought, sold, won or exchanged for anything. FlipUp is not real-money gambling.

What stays on your phone

  • Your poker record (hands played and won, biggest pot, best hand, recent games).
  • Your Range training answers and statistics.
  • Your settings, your name and emoji, your blocked players, and any bots-only table you left open.

None of this is sent to us. "Delete my data" in Settings, or deleting the app, removes it.

What we receive and keep

Only when you host or join an online table or a party:

  • A random device ID. The app makes it on your phone the first time it opens. It proves that a seat is yours. Other players never see it; at a table everyone else sees only a one-way scrambled version.
  • The name and emoji you chose, and your card back if you have Premium. Other players at the table or party see these. Names are checked against a filter.
  • What you do at the table: your bets and other moves, the quick messages you send (chosen from a fixed list; nothing you type is sent), and whether your phone is connected. The table needs this to run the game.
  • A friend code if you use friend features: a short code with your name, emoji and device ID that others can look up.
  • Reports. If you report a player, we keep the reason, the table or party code, the name and emoji shown, and both phones' device IDs, so we can act on it. We read reports and respond within 24 hours.
  • Premium. If you buy FlipUp Premium, Apple handles the payment and never gives us your name, email or card. To unlock Premium on your phone we keep Apple's purchase identifier together with your phone's random device ID.

Camera and contacts

  • The camera is used only to scan a table or party QR code. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Contacts (only if you tap Invite from your contacts) are read on your phone to find a name to message. They are never uploaded.

Who helps us run FlipUp

  • Cloudflare hosts FlipUp's server and the online tables, so it processes the data above on our behalf.
  • Resend sends the email that tells us about a report.
  • Apple handles the App Store, purchases and Restore Purchases.

We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps or websites. FlipUp contains no advertising or analytics code.

Deleting your data

In the app, open your profile, then Settings, then Delete my data. It deletes your friend code from our server, leaves any open table or party, and clears everything FlipUp keeps on your phone, including the device ID. Premium stays with your Apple Account, and Restore Purchases brings it back.

You can also email goodwinconnor007@gmail.com to ask what we hold about your phone or to have anything deleted, including a Premium record or a report you are named in. Include your friend code or a table code if you have one, so we can find it.

Children

FlipUp isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change how FlipUp handles data, we'll update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
