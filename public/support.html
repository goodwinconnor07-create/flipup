FlipUp support

Poker night on your phones. Play chips only, no cash value.

Need a hand? Email support@playflipup.com. Tell us what happened, your iPhone model and the version at the bottom of FlipUp's Settings screen, and we'll get back to you. Reports about a player are answered within 24 hours.

Common questions

How do my friends join my table?

Open Play with friends, then share the game code (or the QR code or link) shown at the top. Your friends open FlipUp, tap Join a game, and type the code or scan the QR code. You can add bots to fill empty seats.

Can I play without friends or without internet?

Yes. Play with bots runs entirely on your phone with no connection. Online tables, parties and joining by code need an internet connection.

Which games does FlipUp have?

No-limit Texas Hold'em plus mini games with their own rules and looks, such as Crazy Pineapple, Crazy Deuces and more. Each game has a How to Play in its sheet. Texas Hold'em is free to host; the mini games need Premium, and only the person hosting needs it.

What does Premium unlock, and how do I restore it?

Premium is a subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through your Apple Account. It unlocks hosting every mini game, the whole bot roster, every Range training hand, saved hands with Replay, share card designs, the gold name and the card backs. To cancel, open your profile, then Settings, then Manage subscription (or the iPhone's Settings app, your name, Subscriptions) at least a day before it renews. If you bought it before, or on another iPhone, open your profile, then Settings, then Restore Purchases, and sign in with the same Apple Account.

Are the chips real money?

No. Chips are play chips. They can't be bought, sold, won or exchanged for anything of value, and FlipUp is not real-money gambling.

What is Range training?

The Train tab shows real hands from published poker strategy breakdowns. You choose the play, then see why it's best, what each play wins on average, and how you're doing over time. Your answers stay on your phone.

Someone is behaving badly at my table. What can I do?

Tap their seat at an online table (or their row on a party's board) to report them, block them, or, if you're the host, remove them. Blocking hides their name and messages on your phone. Reports are read and answered within 24 hours.

How do I delete my data?

Open your profile, then Settings, then Delete my data. It removes what our server keeps about your phone, leaves any open table or party, and clears everything FlipUp keeps on your phone. Details are in the privacy policy. You can also email us and ask.

I can't join a table with the code.

Check the code (codes use letters and numbers, no spaces) and your internet connection. Ask the host to share a fresh link or QR code, or to open a new table. If it keeps failing, email us with the code and what you saw.

What do I need to run FlipUp?

An iPhone with iOS 18 or later. FlipUp is portrait only.
